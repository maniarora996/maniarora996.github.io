Privacy Policy Sudoku — Classic Number Puzzle (package ID: com.maniarora.sudoku) Effective date: September 30, 2026 This policy explains what information Sudoku — Classic Number Puzzle ("the app", "we", "our") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. The app is developed by an independent developer (contact below), not a company. 1. The short version You don't need an account to play. There's no sign-up and no login. Your puzzle progress, stats, and streaks are stored on your own device. We don't operate a server that stores your gameplay. The app shows ads (via Google AdMob) to support development. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to do this — see Section 3. The "Challenge a friend" feature shares a puzzle link through apps you choose (WhatsApp, Email, Facebook, or a copied link). We don't see or store who you send it to. We don't sell your personal information. 2. Information stored on your device To make the game work — difficulty progress, streaks, best times, achievements, unlocked themes, and challenge history — the app stores data locally on your device using standard device storage (Android's app-preferences storage, with a browser-storage fallback in the web build). This data: stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or to any server we operate; is not linked to your name, email, or any personal identifier, because the app doesn't collect one; is removed automatically if you uninstall the app, or if you clear the app's storage from your device settings. 3. Advertising (Google AdMob) The app shows optional rewarded video ads — for example, to unlock an extra hint, an extra mistake allowance, more time in Timed mode, or a fresh attempt at the puzzle you're playing. These are opt-in: an ad only plays if you choose to watch one for a reward. Ads are served through Google AdMob, which may collect and process: your device's advertising identifier and other device/technical identifiers; general, non-precise information such as device type, operating system, and approximate (coarse) region, used to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. AdMob may use this information to personalize ads based on your interests, unless you've limited ad tracking in your device settings. Google's own privacy policy governs how AdMob handles this data: Google Privacy Policy How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services You can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset your advertising ID, from your device's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads). A future paid, ad-free subscription tier is planned. Purchases for it will be processed entirely by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your payment details. 4. Challenge a friend The share feature lets you send a puzzle you're playing to someone else, so you can compare times on the same grid. Here's exactly how it works, because it's designed to need nothing from either of you: The entire puzzle (the starting clues and difficulty) is encoded directly into the link or message itself. Nothing about the puzzle, your identity, or your friend's identity is sent to, or stored on, any server we operate. When you tap WhatsApp, Email, or Facebook in the share sheet, the app hands the message off to that app on your device — it doesn't send anything on your behalf, and doesn't see your contacts or address book. Whatever platform you choose to share through is subject to its own privacy policy, not this one. "Copy link" simply copies text to your device's clipboard. Opening a challenge link or pasting a challenge code someone sent you loads that puzzle locally on your device — again, without any data being sent to us. 5. Themes, challenges, and streaks Themes, daily and weekly challenges, achievements, and streaks are all driven by the locally stored progress described in Section 2. Themes are earned through play — winning five Legend puzzles unlocks them permanently — and nothing about that progress leaves your device. Daily and weekly challenges are generated on your device from the calendar date, so they involve no personal data. 6. Push notifications (planned) A future update may add optional push notifications (for example, a daily reminder) using Firebase Cloud Messaging. If enabled, this would use a device-level notification token to deliver the message — it would not require your name, email, or any other personal detail, and it would be something you can turn off in your device's notification settings at any time. This policy will be updated before that feature ships. 7. Information we don't collect No account, sign-up, or login is required or offered. We don't collect your name, email address, or phone number. We don't access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise (GPS) location. We don't sell personal information to third parties. 8. Your choices Clear your local data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage from your device's app settings — this removes your stats, streaks, and unlocked themes from that device. Limit ad personalization: use your device's ad settings to opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID. Notifications: once push notifications ship, you can disable them any time in your device's notification settings. 11. Data retention Because gameplay data lives on your device rather than on a server we control, it's retained for as long as the app remains installed with that data intact, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Data AdMob or Firebase process is retained according to Google's own retention practices, described in their policies linked above. 12. Changes to this policy We may update this policy as the app's features change — for example, when push notifications or the ad-free subscription go live. The "Effective date" above will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy. 13.
